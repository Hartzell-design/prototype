01

Principles

Four rules every token and component has to follow before it goes into a race.

P—01

Read at 300 km/h

Every number has to read in a 200 ms glance: big, tabular, high-contrast, and always in the same place.

P—02

Motion = Momentum

Animation shows physics, like launching, braking, and drifting. It never exists just for decoration.

P—03

Signal over chrome

Neon is reserved for meaning. Carbon surfaces stay quiet so the signals stay loud.

P—04

Built to evolve

Semantic tokens let a theme, a season, or an AI feature reskin the HUD without rewriting it.

Components

Live, stateful components. Every one is built from tokens above, and the Game UI and Case Study render with these exact components.

Speedometer

sd-gauge

The speed arc, an RPM ring with a redline flash, and gear readout.

Lap Timer

sd-lap

Tabular mono so digits don't jitter. The delta turns green when you beat your best lap and red when you're behind it.

Position

sd-position

Pops on change using --e-snap: green when you gain a place, red when you lose one.

Minimap

sd-minimap

The track spline with the player dot and rivals. It's sampled from a single SVG path.

sd-boost

Skewed segments that charge over time. When the meter is full, the label blinks.

Notifications

sd-toast · 6 types

They slide in on --e-launch and leave on --e-brake. A maximum of three stack in the toast zone.

Spoken and written coaching. It uses the reserved --ai violet, which only machine-generated guidance may use.
